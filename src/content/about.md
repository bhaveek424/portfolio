# About Me

#### Most of the things you (maybe) want to know about me

I'm Bhaveek—a Full Stack Developer who loves building products that feel like magic. Currently shipping AI-powered tools at a B2B startup, while building FastSay (a macOS dictation app) on the side.

I believe great software should be fast, intuitive, and delightful. Whether it's a dashboard handling 50K rows or a chatbot that actually feels human, I obsess over the details.

A Few Things I Believe
- Ship fast, iterate faster. Perfection is the enemy of done.
- The best UI is the one you don't notice. Speed and simplicity win.
- AI is a tool, not a replacement. It makes good engineers great.
- Read source code. The best way to learn is to see how others build.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me:

- Falling down YouTube rabbit holes about how things work
- Occasionally attempting to cook (results vary)
